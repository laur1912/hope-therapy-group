HOPE Is Here To Help

At HOPE Therapy Group, we offer a safe and supportive environment where you and your child can work with a therapist to identify barriers to well-being and develop strategies to overcome them.

Family Relationships

Family is the first place we learn what relationships are and how complicated they can be. In therapy, we identify problems and difficulties in those relationships and the strengths and identity of the family and each member.

Through counseling, your family can learn how to express their thoughts and emotions, and work on building relationships and managing their home life in a healthy way.

As you work with a therapist:

  • Your family will learn how to express thoughts and emotions with one another.
  • You will identify the problems and difficulties in your family relationships, along with the strengths of your family and each member.
  • Your family will work on building relationships and managing home life in a healthy way.
  • Your family will develop the skills needed to navigate life's obstacles effectively.

Anxiety in Children

Perhaps your child is struggling with anxiety and it is impacting their ability to function in daily life. We understand that it can be challenging to know where to start.

Our experienced counselors specialize in helping children with anxiety, and we are here to guide you and your child through the process of learning coping skills and strategies to manage anxiety.

As you work with a therapist:

  • Your child will learn coping skills and strategies to manage anxiety.
  • Your child will have a safe place to explore challenges, with treatment based on their developmental age and need.
  • You will gain new tools to understand and parent your child according to their unique needs.
  • Together, we will identify and address barriers that may be holding your child back.

Anger Outbursts

Maybe you've noticed an increase in your child's anger outbursts and want to help them better understand and regulate their emotions.

Our therapists are experts in helping children learn to communicate their feelings effectively and develop healthy ways to manage their anger. Seeking counseling shows your deep care for their growth, and with guidance, they can navigate difficult phases and succeed.

As you work with a therapist:

  • Your child will gain insight into the sources of their anger and develop an understanding of their typical responses to it.
  • Your child will develop better communication skills to express their feelings without pushing others away.
  • Your child will develop strategies to calm down when they feel triggered and learn to express anger in a healthier way.
  • Your family will enjoy more positive and fulfilling relationships with one another.

These are a few of the most common reasons families reach out, but they are not a complete list of what our team can help with. Our therapists also work with depression, grief and loss, trauma, LGBTQI concerns, and more. Please feel free to reach out to our intake team with your unique needs.

FAQs

What can we expect on our first visit?

Your therapist will go through an assessment that reviews what issues, symptoms, and difficulties led you to seek out therapy services. It allows your therapist to get to know you and your history to see if the therapeutic relationship will be a good fit. You can choose your provider and discuss questions, concerns, and feedback with your therapist.

TherapyPortal, a safe and encrypted platform for storing confidential information, will send you the intake forms. After scheduling an initial appointment, you’ll receive an email with instructions to create a login.

How long and frequent will therapy sessions be?

Sessions are 50-60 minutes long. The frequency of therapy sessions will be up to you to decide with your therapist. Generally, in beginning therapy, you can expect sessions to be weekly or once every other week in order to build consistency toward resolving your symptoms or difficulties.

What can family therapy help with?

Family therapy explores how each member plays a vital role in the family and seeks to improve interactions in a safe space. Through counseling, your family can learn how to express their thoughts and emotions, and work on building relationships and managing their home life in a healthy way. Our team also works with concerns such as anxiety, anger, depression, grief and loss, and trauma. This is not an exhaustive list, so please feel free to reach out to our intake team with your unique needs.

Do you also offer therapy for children and adults?

Yes. Children's therapy is helpful when a child or adolescent needs a safe place to explore challenges, and treatment is based on developmental age and need. We also offer adult counseling services to support individuals in finding balance and fulfillment in their own lives.

How do we find the right therapist for our family?

Our Clinician Search Tool lets you narrow your search by specialty and by the specific issues you wish to address. You can click on each therapist's photo to read their biography, then complete our contact form. You can also reach out to our intake team, and we will match you or your loved ones with a therapist who is the best fit for your specific needs and goals.

Can we do family therapy online?

Yes. Telehealth is available throughout California, and in person sessions are available at our San Diego and Los Angeles offices. Please note that we cannot schedule appointments by walking into the office, as we do not have front desk staff.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. If you have one of the insurances listed above, it is important for you to verify your mental health benefits so you understand your coverage prior to your appointment. Some insurance companies require a prior authorization before the first appointment, or they will not cover the costs of services.

In addition, we do not bill secondary insurance or accept Medicare or Medicaid plans.

What is a superbill?

A superbill is a document that lists the healthcare services provided by a professional along with the associated fees. Clients can use this document as an invoice to request payment from their insurance provider for services received outside of their network.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations or missed appointments without 24-hour notice will be subject to a $75.00 fee.
